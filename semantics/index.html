Data Semantics

What the data means — not just what it looks like. Typed primitives, invariants, and relationships for the TPC-H supply chain model.

Why this matters

A data catalog tells you what exists (tables, columns, types). Semantics tells you what it means:

  • Types — Is this an Entity, Fact, Dimension, or Relationship?
  • Invariants — What constraints must hold for the data to be correct?
  • Morphisms — How do transforms change structure? What's preserved?
  • Relationships — Not just foreign keys — enrichments, aggregations, refinements.

This layer lets AI assistants reason about your data, not just list columns.
