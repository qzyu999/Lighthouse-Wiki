Invariants

Business rules that must hold for data to be correct.

What Are Invariants

Invariants are conditions that must always be true for a dataset to be considered valid. They encode business logic as testable assertions.

Examples

A customer_id must never be null. An order total must be non-negative. A created_at timestamp must not be in the future.

Enforcement

Invariants are checked at write time in the silver layer and again as post-conditions after gold layer transformations complete.
