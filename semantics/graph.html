TPC-H Entity-Relationship Diagram

The TPC-H schema is a snowflake schema centered on the lineitem fact table. Below is an ASCII representation of the full entity-relationship diagram showing all foreign key relationships.

Simplified Relationship Flow


region ◄─── nation ◄─── customer ◄─── orders ◄─── lineitem ──► part
                │                                       │            │
                └──► supplier ◄────────────────── partsupp ◄────────┘
                                                  (ps_partkey, ps_suppkey)

Read left-to-right as "belongs to" and right-to-left as "contains many." The lineitem table sits at the center, joining to orders (via l_orderkey), part (via l_partkey), and supplier (via l_suppkey). The partsupp bridge table connects parts to their suppliers with availability and cost information.

Edge Descriptions

From To FK Column Cardinality Meaning
nation region n_regionkey N:1 Every nation belongs to exactly one region (e.g., FRANCE → EUROPE)
customer nation c_nationkey N:1 Every customer is domiciled in one nation
supplier nation s_nationkey N:1 Every supplier is located in one nation
orders customer o_custkey N:1 Every order is placed by one customer
lineitem orders l_orderkey N:1 Every line item belongs to one order (avg ~4 items/order)
lineitem partsupp l_partkey + l_suppkey N:1 Every line item references a specific part-supplier combination
partsupp part ps_partkey N:1 Each part-supplier record references one part
partsupp supplier ps_suppkey N:1 Each part-supplier record references one supplier

Graph Properties

  • Schema Type: Snowflake (multi-level dimension hierarchy: region → nation → customer/supplier)
  • Central Fact: lineitem (highest cardinality, most foreign keys)
  • Bridge Table: partsupp (resolves the many-to-many between part and supplier)
  • Hierarchy Depth: Maximum 4 hops from lineitem to region
  • Total Tables: 8 (1 fact, 1 bridge, 6 dimensions)
