Metrics

KPI definitions, formulas, and source tables.

Overview

Metrics are named calculations with a defined formula, grain, and source table. They provide a single source of truth for KPIs across the TPC-H supply chain dataset. Each metric below is the canonical definition — dashboards, reports, and LLM-generated queries should reference these formulas to ensure consistency.

Metric Registry

Metric Formula Source Granularity
revenue SUM(l_extendedprice * (1 - l_discount)) lineitem Per line item (aggregatable to order, customer, date)
discounted_revenue SUM(l_extendedprice * (1 - l_discount) * (1 + l_tax)) lineitem Per line item (includes tax impact)
avg_order_value AVG(o_totalprice) orders Per order (often grouped by segment or date)
fill_rate COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE o_orderstatus = 'F') / COUNT(*) orders Per order (ratio, 0.0–1.0)
avg_discount AVG(l_discount) lineitem Per line item (often grouped by supplier or part)
supplier_concentration COUNT(DISTINCT l_suppkey) lineitem Per part (measures supply diversity)

Usage Notes

Revenue vs. Discounted Revenue: The revenue metric represents the net amount collected (price after discount). The discounted_revenue metric adds tax back in, representing the total charged amount including tax. Most financial reports use revenue; tax-inclusive analyses use discounted_revenue.

Fill Rate Interpretation: A fill rate of 0.72 means 72% of orders have been fully shipped. This is an operational health metric — declining fill rate signals fulfillment bottlenecks.

Supplier Concentration: Low values (1–2 suppliers per part) indicate supply chain risk. This metric is typically filtered to specific part types or regions when used in risk dashboards.

Example Queries

-- Revenue by market segment (TPC-H Q3 simplified)
SELECT c.c_mktsegment,
       SUM(l.l_extendedprice * (1 - l.l_discount)) AS revenue
FROM   lineitem l
JOIN   orders o ON l.l_orderkey = o.o_orderkey
JOIN   customer c ON o.o_custkey = c.c_custkey
WHERE  o.o_orderdate < DATE '1995-03-15'
GROUP BY c.c_mktsegment
ORDER BY revenue DESC;

-- Fill rate trend by month
SELECT DATE_TRUNC('month', o_orderdate) AS month,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE o_orderstatus = 'F')::FLOAT / COUNT(*) AS fill_rate
FROM   orders
GROUP BY 1
ORDER BY 1;
