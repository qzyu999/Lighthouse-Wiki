Known Issues

Active data quality problems, their impact, and workarounds.

Active

orders.total_amount includes tax

Since the 2024-03-01 migration, total_amount includes sales tax. Previously it was subtotal only.

Impact: Revenue calculations ~8% higher than historical comparisons.

Workaround: Use total_amount / 1.08 for approximate subtotal.

Affected: gold.orders, daily_revenue

customer_email NULL for legacy records

Records before 2022-01-01 have NULL customer_email.

Workaround: Filter by created_at >= '2022-01-01' or use COALESCE.

Resolved

Duplicate orders in Silver layer

Resolved 2024-06-15. Added dedup step on (order_id, event_timestamp).
