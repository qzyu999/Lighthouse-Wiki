Troubleshooting

Common issues and how to resolve them.

Connection Failures

Verify your network connectivity, credentials, and that the endpoint URL is correct. Check if the service is currently healthy on the status page.

Stale Data

If query results seem outdated, check the pipeline status for the relevant table. A failed or delayed pipeline run may be the cause.

Permission Errors

Access is granted per-table and per-schema. If you receive a permissions error, request access through your team lead or the data governance channel.
