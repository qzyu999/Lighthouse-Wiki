Medallion Layers

Bronze, Silver, Gold — progressive data refinement.

Bronze Layer

Raw ingestion from source systems. Data is stored as-is with minimal transformation, preserving the original schema and fidelity.

Silver Layer

Cleansed and conformed data. Deduplication, type casting, null handling, and schema enforcement are applied at this stage.

Gold Layer

Business-ready tables optimized for consumption. Aggregations, joins, and dimensional modeling produce the final datasets used by analysts and dashboards.
