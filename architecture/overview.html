Architecture Overview

How the platform is designed, end to end.

System Design

This page describes the high-level architecture of the data platform, including the major components and how they interact.

Components

The platform consists of ingestion pipelines, a multi-layer lakehouse, a semantic layer, and consumption interfaces for analysts and downstream services.

Data Flow

Data moves from source systems through bronze (raw), silver (cleansed), and gold (business-ready) layers before being exposed via metrics and catalog endpoints.
